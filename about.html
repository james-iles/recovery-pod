Contrast Therapy

The Power of Heat & Cold

Alternating between sauna heat and cold plunge activates your body's natural recovery systems. The contrast drives circulation, reduces inflammation, relieves muscle soreness, and leaves you feeling deeply restored.

Why Contrast Therapy?

Science-backed benefits for body and mind

Sauna Image

Swedish Sauna

Traditional dry heat between 80–100°C relaxes muscles, promotes deep sweating, and triggers the release of endorphins. Regular sauna use supports cardiovascular health, improves sleep quality, and helps the body detoxify naturally.

Cold Plunge Image

Cold Plunge

Immersion in cold water between 3–7°C triggers vasoconstriction, reduces inflammation, and stimulates the vagus nerve. Cold exposure builds mental resilience, boosts metabolism, and accelerates muscle recovery after exercise.

How to Prepare

Everything you need to know before your visit

Swim Attire

Arrive in suitable swim and sauna attire. We want you to be comfortable throughout your session.

Bring Towels

Bring a towel to dry yourself and a towel to sit on in the sauna. Towels are also available to hire on site.

Water Bottle

Bring a water bottle — you will be able to fill this on site. Staying hydrated is essential during contrast therapy.

Shower Before

Please shower before you arrive. This keeps the water clean and enhances your experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

What is contrast therapy?

Contrast therapy involves alternating between hot (sauna) and cold (plunge pool) exposure. This cycle of heating and cooling stimulates blood flow, reduces muscle soreness, and promotes overall recovery. A typical session involves multiple rounds of heat and cold.

How long is a session?

Sessions typically last 60 minutes, giving you time for multiple rounds of sauna and cold plunge with rest periods in between. We recommend 3–4 rounds for the best results.

Is cold plunge safe for everyone?

Cold plunge is generally safe for healthy individuals. However, if you have a heart condition, are pregnant, have Raynaud's disease, or have any medical concerns, please consult your doctor before your first session. We are always happy to discuss any questions you may have.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, we operate on a booking-only basis to ensure a relaxed, uncrowded experience. You can book your session through our online booking page.

What should I bring?

Bring swim attire, a towel (or hire one from us), a water bottle, and any toiletries you need for after your session. We provide showers and a changing area.

Where are you located?

We are based in Bristol. Our exact address and directions will be shared when you make a booking.
